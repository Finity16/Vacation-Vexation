# Vacation Vexation

Vacation Vexation is a short WarioWare-style game developed for Hack Club Stardance! It tests your reflexes across a few fun vacation-themed minigames.

## Features
- **Platformer minigame** Quickly navigate the platforms to collect 3 pieces of clothing to pack in your bag
- **Clicker minigame** Rapidly tap 4 items on the screen
- **Timer:** Every single minigame must be completed in exactly 10 seconds to keep the pressure on.
- **Life System:** You start with 5 lives (represented by our lovely plane tickets!). Lose them all, and it's game over.
- **Assets:** All sprites, backgrounds, and user interface elements were custom-drawn by me.

## Development Details
This was my very first time building a project in the Godot Engine using GDScript, and I had a lot of fun. The UI was pretty confusing at first, but I think I've gotten a handle on it! 

## How to Play
Since this is an HTML5 web export, you can play the game directly in your browser without downloading anything! Simply click "Start Game" on the title screen. Use your arrow keys and space bar to move during the platforming sections and your mouse to click targets during the tapping minigames.
